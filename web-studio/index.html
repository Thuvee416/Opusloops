Arrangement automation

No envelope points

Volume: 0–1. Pan: −1 left, 0 center, +1 right. Click the graph to add; drag points to move. Envelopes follow Arrangement time and are included in WAV exports. Record automation captures supported mixer and device controls. Latch holds the last value until Finish or Stop. Touch writes while adjusting a control and resumes its envelope on release. Later loop passes overwrite the played span; untouched parameters stay unchanged. Outside recording, moving an automated control overrides its envelope until re-enabled. Device, return and send levels are available in Parameter. Sends follow their Pre-fader setting. Reverb decay and saturation drive are not yet automatable.

Shift+wheel or horizontal scrolling pans; Ctrl/Cmd+wheel zooms around the pointer. Select time, then use Shift+Left/Right to extend its moving edge by a quarter beat. Focus a region: Up/Down selects across tracks; Home/End selects the first/last clip. Left/Right moves by a quarter beat, Ctrl/Cmd+D duplicates, 0 activates/deactivates, Delete removes. Drag regions to move them; hold Alt/Option before dragging to duplicate; drag their edges to trim. Select for precise values, splitting, or duplication. Click a bar number to seek. Pause retains position; Stop returns to bar 1. Record replaces the previous take.

Your first session

  1. Launch a scene at the right of the clip grid, or launch individual clips.
  2. Press the red record button to capture a fresh Session performance. Stop when you are done.
  3. Switch to Arrangement and press Play. Select regions to edit their timing.
  4. Save a project file with embedded audio, or export a stereo WAV.

Space Play / stop   Tab Switch views when not editing a field.

In the piano roll, Shift-click or Shift-drag selects notes. With the piano roll focused, use ⌘/Ctrl A, C, X, V to select all, copy, cut, or paste; Delete removes the selection. The internal note clipboard stays within this browser tab.

Imported loops default to Repitch. Choose Stretch in Audio clip controls to preserve pitch as tempo changes, with independent pitch adjustment. Set the loop's beat length to match the file. MIDI clips use a simple built-in synth. Mixer and supported effect automation can be edited or recorded in Arrangement. Desktop plug-ins and editable warp markers are not included in this prototype.

Autosave is local to this browser and can be cleared by the browser. Download a project file for a durable copy. This is an independent studio, not an Ableton product.
