# Touch Sequencer

The sequencer is the main **Studio** surface. Its four rows are Kick, Snare, Bass, and Chords. Sixteen columns form one repeating pattern.

## Add and remove steps

- Tap an empty cell to enable it.
- Tap an active cell to disable it.
- The playhead highlight shows the step currently being heard.
- **Mute** on a row silences it without changing the pattern.

Edits can be made while the loop is playing. They take effect on the running pattern without opening another editor.

## Tempo and playback

Use the central transport to play or pause the loop. The minus and plus buttons change tempo in two-BPM steps from 56 to 180 BPM. Tapping the key cycles through the available minor keys.

## Refine a pattern

**Refine this loop** changes the current pattern from a short instruction. The quick choices make it sparser, busier, warmer, or surprising. Refinement creates a variation locally; it does not send the project to an external AI provider.

## Work in short passes

Build one part, listen, then add another. Muting a row in the mixer is often faster than deleting steps when deciding whether a part belongs.

## Built-in audio

Opusloops synthesizes its four voices with Web Audio. External VST, Audio Unit, LV2, and desktop plugin formats are not supported by the mobile production app.

## Avoid accidental loss

Save after a useful change. Sign in for editable project sync, or export a four-bar WAV when you need a playable audio file. See [Projects and offline use](projects-offline.md).
